## Hi ✋

I'm Paul Martin, a Microprocessor Technology & Instrumentation graduate from the University of Nairobi and a former CERN Summer Student. I love working on projects that revolve around the intersection of physics, electronics and computer science.

My interests span particle detector instrumentation, scientific computing, machine learning, embedded systems and interactive 3D simulation.

I enjoy understanding how systems work from first principles — from particle interactions and low-level hardware to intelligent algorithms and virtual environments.

 **Research:** Muon tomography, atmospheric muons, cosmic-ray showers, and particle detector simulations.
 **Machine Learning:** Variational Autoencoders, time-series anomaly detection, and scientific data analysis.
 **Hardware:** Embedded systems, FPGA development, digital logic and instrumentation.
 **Currently building:** Vehicle physics, interactive environments and 3D assets using Unreal Engine 5 and Blender.
 **Open to:** Research collaborations, postgraduate opportunities and challenging engineering projects.

##  Research Experience

### CERN — ATLAS DCS (FPGAs & Machine Learning)

- Machine learning-based anomaly detection for an FPGA-based SoC, the Embedded Monitoring System (EMP).
- Variational Autoencoders for multivariate time-series sensor data.
- FPGA development, VHDL on VIVADO.

### Muon Tomography & Cosmic-Ray shower Simulations

- Simulation of atmospheric muon flux and particle distributions.
- Extensive air shower simulations using ARTI and CORSIKA.
- Muon detector simulation and characterization.
- Scientific data processing, visualization and computational modelling.
- Containerized simulation environments using Docker.
